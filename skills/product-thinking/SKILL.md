# Product thinking (office-hours lite)

Adapted from gstack `/office-hours`. Do not write code until the user approves scope.

## Six questions

Ask or answer:

1. Who feels the pain (specific role/person)?
2. What do they do today without this feature?
3. What is the smallest useful slice for the next PR?
4. How will we know it worked (one observable outcome)?
5. What assumptions must we validate first?
6. What is explicitly out of scope?

## Output

- **Problem** (one paragraph)
- **Narrowest first PR** (bullet list)
- **Deferred** (what not to build yet)
- Then offer `/plan` — do not implement until asked

## When to escalate

If the request is a large greenfield app, recommend the human read [product-thinking-before-code.md](../../docs/knowledge-guides/product-thinking-before-code.md) and discuss with a mentor before autoplan-style tooling.
